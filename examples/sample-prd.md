# PRD: PetPantry Vet-Diet Match

**Author:** Sample PM
**Status:** Draft v1 — for review
**Date:** May 2026

> **Note:** PetPantry is a fictional product invented for the prd-critic skill demo. Any resemblance to real subscription services is coincidental.

---

## 1. Problem

Owners of dogs with chronic conditions (allergies, pancreatitis, kidney disease, diabetes) struggle to keep their pets on the right food. Vets prescribe a therapeutic diet, but owners then face a fragmented buying experience — therapeutic food is sold at the clinic at high markup, online with confusing variants, or at speciality retailers with limited stock. Many lapse off the diet within three months, which is bad for the dog and undoes the vet's clinical plan.

We will launch **PetPantry Vet-Diet Match**: a subscription that delivers the right therapeutic food on schedule, priced below clinic markup, with the vet's recommendation captured at sign-up.

## 2. Goals

- Improve adherence to therapeutic diets
- Build a defensible position in the pet-health market
- Open a new revenue stream for PetPantry beyond mainstream pet food

## 3. Target users

Dog owners in the US whose vet has prescribed a therapeutic diet. Estimated TAM: ~3M dogs in the US on a vet-prescribed therapeutic diet at any one time.

## 4. Scope

In v1 we will:

- Let users enter their dog's diagnosis and vet's recommended food at sign-up
- Match against our catalogue of 40 SKUs across the four major therapeutic brands
- Deliver on a 4-week cycle, with reminder and skip-month options
- Offer a "vet portal" where vets can submit recommendations on behalf of their patients

We will support all 50 US states at launch.

## 5. Success metrics

- Increase user engagement by launch + 90 days
- Build a strong vet partnership channel
- Hit revenue targets for FY27

## 6. Pricing

Subscription pricing will be ~15% below the equivalent clinic markup. We project this will support a healthy contribution margin.

## 7. Timeline

- **Month 1–2:** Build catalogue and matching logic
- **Month 3:** Beta with 100 users
- **Month 4:** Public launch
- **Month 5+:** Scale
